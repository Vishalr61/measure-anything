# Absurd unit assumptions (v1)

These units are **deterministic, offline, and data-driven**. Each absurd unit maps to a canonical base unit per category:

- length → meter
- mass → kilogram
- time → second
- volume → liter
- temperature → (normal-only in v1; handled separately)

The constants below are intentionally **approximate** (order-of-magnitude / typical consumer items) but not “made up.”

## Length (meters)
- **banana**: 0.19 m (≈ 19 cm typical banana length)
- **fridge**: 1.7 m (typical fridge height)
- **bus**: 12.0 m (typical city bus length)
- **blue_whale**: 24.0 m (adult blue whale length, rough)

## Mass (kilograms)
- **cat**: 4.5 kg (average adult domestic cat)
- **bowling_ball**: 6.8 kg (15 lb class bowling ball)
- **microwave**: 12.0 kg (typical countertop microwave)
- **elephant**: 6000 kg (adult elephant, rough)

## Time (seconds)
- **coffee_break**: 900 s (15 min)
- **gym_session**: 3600 s (60 min)
- **one_episode**: 2700 s (45 min)
- **bad_meeting**: 5400 s (90 min)

## Volume (liters)
- **soda_can**: 0.355 L (355 mL)
- **wine_glass**: 0.15 L (150 mL typical pour)
- **bathtub**: 150 L (typical fill, rough)
- **bucket**: 10 L (common bucket)
